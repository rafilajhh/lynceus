# Reference gate SOP — RDF receiving (reference SOP, not a real plant's document)

This is a reference procedure written for the Lynceus demo. It is not a document of any real plant. Its figures come only from docs/research/realcase-indonesia.md, and each section names its source inline.

## SOP-RDF-2.1 — Retake the load photo
The gate record starts with a check of the waste condition as the truck enters, one of the six fields in the PUPR TPA receiving SOP (Rakernis Adipura 2019). A photo the vision model cannot read with confidence does not satisfy that check. An arm roll truck carries about 5.2 t in a 14 m³ container (372.71 kg/m³, ITATS Surabaya), so the whole surface must be visible from above. Retake the photo from the top platform, and if it is still unreadable, hand the load to the supervisor under SOP-RDF-5.2.

## SOP-RDF-2.2 — Collect missing inputs
The PUPR TPA receiving SOP (Rakernis Adipura 2019) records six fields at the gate: plate number, driver, entry time, waste source, weight or volume from the weighbridge, and the waste condition check. Lynceus will not issue an accept or reject verdict when a mandatory input such as the photo or the waybill text is absent. Collect each missing input at the gate, using the field list of the demo waybill skeleton (docs/research/realcase-indonesia.md section 4d). Then re-run the inspection from the start.

## SOP-RDF-3.2 — Re-weigh the truck
Weighbridge practice records gross (bruto), tare (tarra) and net (netto) weight, as at the TPA Benowo weighbridge (ITATS Surabaya). Typical net loads are about 7.4 t for a compactor and about 5.2 t for an arm roll (ITATS Surabaya). A weight far from the waybill figure is therefore either a loading problem or a paperwork problem. National rules cap axle load at 8 t per axle (PP 55/2012), and UU 22/2009 Article 169 sets a fine of up to Rp 500.000 for overloading. Hold the ticket and re-weigh before any other step.

## SOP-RDF-3.3 — Verify the waybill with the driver
The waybill is the supplier's claim, and the weighbridge and the photo are the plant's evidence. The demo waybill skeleton carries the plate number, driver, source, destination, gross, tare, net and load type (docs/research/realcase-indonesia.md section 4d, from the PUPR TPA receiving SOP). When a field disagrees with the evidence, ask the driver to confirm it at the gate. Correct the ticket if the driver can document the right value, and void it if not.

## SOP-RDF-4.1 — Do not tip a rejected load
Metal is 3.11% of national waste by weight (SIPSN 2025), so a visible metal item in an RDF load is plausible and must be taken seriously. A shredder unit is priced at Rp 350 jt (RDI 2024, Lampiran 2A) and its economic life is 5 years. Unplanned kiln downtime is benchmarked at about US$28,000 per hour, roughly Rp 450 juta at Rp 16.000/USD (oxmaint, section 5a). Keep the truck on the weighbridge and do not tip, because a tipped load cannot be taken back out of the RDF stream.

## SOP-RDF-4.2 — Remove hard contaminants at the sorting bay
The load goes to the sorting bay so that the named hard contaminants (metal, rock, concrete, tire, e-waste) are removed by hand or machine. Metal is 3.11% and glass 2.39% of national waste by weight (SIPSN 2025). At the Cilacap RDF plant about 15% of the input mass is discarded as inert material (SWITCH-Asia SBI case study), so removal is normal plant work. A bag opener costs Rp 150-200 jt per unit (RDI 2024), which is the equipment this load must not reach.

## SOP-RDF-4.3 — Photograph the load and notify the supplier
The photo and the inspection record number are the evidence for the supplier notice. The PUPR TPA receiving SOP (Rakernis Adipura 2019) already tells the gate officer to refuse hazardous, medical and industrial waste and to record the waste source. RDF is bought at Rp 300.000 to Rp 450.000 per tonne (ASI, SWITCH-Asia and RDI 2024), so a load that must be refused or reworked is a commercial dispute and not only a plant problem. Notify the supplier named on the waybill, quoting the record number.

## SOP-RDF-4.4 — Tip to the pre-sort line
Light contaminants such as plastic film, glass and textile are ordinary parts of Indonesian waste, at 20.52% plastic, 2.39% glass and 2.51% textile nationally (SIPSN 2025). They are handled by pre-processing, which is why the Cilacap plant shreds and screens its input and obtains about 50% of the input mass as RDF (SWITCH-Asia SBI case study). A load with light contaminants goes to the pre-sort line and not direct feed. Direct feed would skip the step that turns mixed waste into a fuel of about 3.000 kcal/kg (SWITCH-Asia).

## SOP-RDF-5.1 — Take a grab sample for moisture
Municipal waste in Indonesia carries 55-60% moisture at Cilacap (SWITCH-Asia SBI case study) and about 65% in the Damanhuri (ITB) study. That is why the Cilacap plant needs a 21 day biological drying step before the material can be burned. A waybill that claims a dry load while the photo shows a wet one is therefore a real conflict. Hold the ticket, and let the supervisor take a grab sample so that moisture is measured and not guessed.

## SOP-RDF-5.2 — Supervisor manual visual check
Lynceus gives a screening estimate, and the final call on an unclear load belongs to a person. The PUPR TPA receiving SOP (Rakernis Adipura 2019) already includes a recording officer and a traffic officer at the gate, so a supervisor check fits the existing gate staffing. The supervisor looks at the load directly and decides to accept, escalate or reject. The supervisor's decision is recorded in the audit record next to the AI verdict.

## SOP-RDF-5.3 — Apply the contract moisture penalty
Moisture is a cost and not only a quality flag. A wet load needs the 21 day biological drying step described at Cilacap (SWITCH-Asia SBI case study). RDF is priced at Rp 300.000 to Rp 450.000 per tonne (ASI, SWITCH-Asia and RDI 2024), and RDF made from fresh waste has a higher calorific value than RDF from old waste (RDI 2024, section 3.3.2). The penalty rate itself is set by the supply contract and not by this reference SOP, so apply it only after the grab sample confirms the moisture.

## SOP-RDF-6.1 — Tip to the RDF storage bay and release the ticket
A load that passes every gate is tipped at the RDF storage bay and its ticket is released. RDF is only about 5% of the fuel tonnage in a typical cement kiln (RDI 2024, Bab 3), so each accepted load supports a small but real substitution target. Semen Indonesia Group reached a thermal substitution rate of 7.27% in 2023 (Kompas), and SBI reached 11.73% in 2022 (SBI sustainability report 2022). Record the gross, tare and net weight on the released ticket.

## SOP-RDF-7.1 — Gas alert
A strongly anaerobic load is a sign of spoiling organic matter, and food waste is 40.77% of national waste by weight (SIPSN 2025). RDF made from old waste loses calorific value as the organics decompose (RDI 2024, section 3.3.2), and the waste moisture at Cilacap is 55-60% (SWITCH-Asia). When the gas gate fails, ventilate the tipping area before unloading and keep the cab closed. The research basis gives no numeric gas limit, so the alert threshold is a Lynceus gate parameter and not a figure from these sources.
